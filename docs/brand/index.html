Clear space and minimum size

¼¼

Keep a quarter of the mark's width clear on every side.

Rules

  • Clear space: ¼ of the mark width on every side (16 units of the 64-unit grid); for the lockup, measure from the mark.
  • Full mark: 40 px minimum. Below that, use the small mark.
  • Small mark / favicon: 16 px minimum; 24-32 px in the top bar.
  • Lockup: 28 px tall minimum, so the wordmark stays at 11 px or more.
  • Backgrounds: the main files on #0F0F12 or surfaces of the dark theme; the -light files on white or the light theme; the app icon tile anywhere.
  • Color: never recolor the mark outside the two variants; a single-color version is currentColor only for print or embossing.

Decoration only

The raw brand cyan and violet are too light for text on white, and brand indigo disappears on night. Use them for the logo, illustrations and fills, not for text.

Icons

One monochrome set: 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, 2 px corner radius, currentColor. <Icon name="..." size? title? /> in frontend/src/components/Icon.tsx. Each cell shows 24 px and 16 px.

Dark background

A static particle network (bg-particles.svg, 800×600, seamless) over #0F0F12 with two soft radial glows done in CSS. Text always sits on a surface, never on the particles; hidden under prefers-reduced-motion and in light mode.

Cache-aside, write-through and eviction policies, with two research notes attached.

Readable over the glow

Body #ECECF1 on surface #17171D is 15.16:1; muted #9D9DAE is 6.69:1. The glow peaks at about 10% alpha, so contrast stays above 13:1 even on the bare background.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the small mark at 32 px and below, the full mark from 40 px.
  • Pick the variant by background: main files on dark, -light files on light.
  • Keep icons at 1.5 px on the 24 grid; scale the whole icon, not the stroke.
  • Put text on --surface panels over the particle background.
  • Use the accent token, not the raw brand hex, for interactive UI.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows and glows to the mark.
  • Put letters in the mark, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Use cyan or violet text on white, or indigo on the night background.
  • Animate the particle background, or raise its opacity.
  • Mix in filled or 2 px icons from another set.
